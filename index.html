About The Gym

Built By Lifters, For Lifters

FORGE opened in 2014 in a converted textile warehouse with four barbells and a single squat rack. Today it's the same idea, just scaled up: a no-nonsense strength floor, coaches who actually coach, and programming built around your numbers -- not a one-size-fits-all class.

We believe fitness should be measured in progress, not in how sore you feel. Every member gets a baseline assessment, a written program, and a coach who tracks your lifts week over week.

  • Free movement assessment for every new member
  • Small-group coaching, max 10 lifters per session
  • In-house physiotherapist on site three days a week
